# Commercial General Liability (Sample)

> SAMPLE CORPUS — synthetic demonstration data for BrokerIQ. Not legal advice.

## Coverage Triggers

CGL policies respond to bodily injury and property damage caused by an
"occurrence" — an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to
substantially the same general harmful conditions. Claims-made trigger only
applies where the policy explicitly attaches coverage to claims first made
during the policy period.

## Contractual Liability

The contractual liability insuring agreement covers liability the insured
assumes under contract, but only for contracts that qualify as "insured
contracts" under the policy. Indemnification clauses that shift liability
without a written contract fall outside coverage.

## Products and Completed Operations

Products coverage applies to bodily injury or property damage arising out of
the insured's product after it has left the insured's possession. Completed
operations extends to work that is finished. Both carry their own aggregate
limits that are separate from the general aggregate.

## Pollution Exclusion

The standard pollution exclusion is broad and excludes most pollutant
discharges, including gradual ones. Coverage can be restored via a dedicated
pollution liability policy — a common gap for manufacturers and property
managers, and a frequent post-claim dispute.

## Employment Practices Exposure

CGL does not cover employment practices liability (harassment, wrongful
termination, discrimination). Employment practices liability insurance (EPLI)
is written separately and is increasingly expected by M&A diligence teams
even for small employers.
